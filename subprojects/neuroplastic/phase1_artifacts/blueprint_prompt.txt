The A_log tensor controls how fast each SSM head's state decays between tokens. Negative A_log means fast decay (short memory). Positive A_log means slow decay or growth (long memory). At baseline, A_log values range from about -7.5 to +7.5 with a mean near -1.3, meaning most heads default to moderate-to-fast decay. The D tensor is a direct skip connection bypass, with values ranging roughly 0 to 26 at baseline, providing strong residual paths in some heads. These two tensors together determine whether Mamba heads operate as integrators or as rapidly-forgetting local detectors.

Mamba layers have O(L) complexity in sequence length. They do not compute explicit token-to-token attention scores.
